My value proposition

My biggest advantage is the combination of creative and technical skills.

A lot of people enjoy coming up with ideas. Others enjoy building things. Others are good at presenting them. I’m working on becoming good at all three.

That means I can come up with an idea, figure out how to make it real, and communicate it in a way that people actually care about.

I’m also constantly learning. I don’t expect to already know everything—I want to become the person who can figure it out.

  1. Idea Notice it. Sketch it.
  2. Build Model it. Print it. Wire it.
  3. Communicate Perform it. Present it. Sell it.

The Brand

Name design and personal logo.

MCHAEL

Create • Perform • Build

Name design

My name design uses bold, geometric capital letters to represent confidence, structure, and a modern approach to creativity.

The electric-blue accent connects the design to technology and makes the name recognizable without making it unnecessarily complicated.

I chose a clean design because I want my personal brand to communicate that I focus on ideas and execution rather than unnecessary decoration.

Structure + Creativity

Personal logo

My logo combines my initials, MP, with an incomplete square.

The square represents building, structure, technology, and design. One section is intentionally left open. That represents creativity and the idea that I don’t want to be boxed into one skill or category.

Together, the logo represents: Structure + Creativity.

  • M + P — Michael Petrenko
  • Square — structure, building, technology
  • Open section — creativity, refusing one box
